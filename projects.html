3D · ENGINEERING · WEB APP2026

WireLab PH

Browser 3D house designer and wiring lab for Filipino electrical engineers. Lay out rooms, ~100 furniture, architecture, people and vehicle models at any size Tinkercad-style, place 25 kinds of electrical devices, wire daisy-chained circuits in conduit, and a Philippine Electrical Code engine computes the load schedule, breaker and wire sizes, per-segment voltage drop and a materials list. Includes AutoCAD-style drafting (command line, OSNAP/ORTHO, layers), a Minecraft-style inventory with rotating 3D previews, a construction materials estimate, editable floor and circuit plans, and PDF export at true scale on A4–A0, ARCH and Long bond paper. Built backward from a spec, with 115 automated tests.

AI · TRANSPORT · WEB APP2026

SaanSakay

AI commute guide for Metro Manila. Ask in Taglish and a custom multimodal routing engine (1,700+ jeep, bus, LRT and MRT routes) returns the fastest, cheapest and fewest-transfer options with official LTFRB fares and student, senior and PWD discounts. Routes are snapped to real roads, and live tracking glides along them with a 1 km "Para" countdown before your stop. It also has an overcharge checker and ETAs that learn from finished trips, on a custom vector map with no paid APIs.
