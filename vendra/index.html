How Vendra works

Vendra helps small businesses in the Philippines turn customer questions into bookings, orders, quotations and service requests. It replies in English, Tagalog or Taglish.

AI with guardrails

The assistant only calls controlled tools: business info, catalog, live availability and draft preparation. It cannot book, change prices, give discounts or verify payments.

Customer confirms

Every submission goes form → review → confirm, with duplicate-safe keys. Changed prices or times require reconfirmation.

Staff in control

Discounts, complaints and payment claims hand off to staff. The AI pauses until someone resumes it. Owner, manager and staff permissions are enforced on the server.

Integrity under load

Bookings use locked availability checks, so there are no double bookings. Stock is reserved on order and deducted once at handover. Quotations are versioned and need owner approval.

In the full app

  • Multi-business tenancy with TOTP MFA for owners, audit logs, retention jobs and CSV exports.
  • Modules for appointments, product orders, quotations and service requests, plus reports and team schedules.
  • Server-side Gemini adapter with function calling, an offline rules engine, monthly usage limits and fallbacks.
  • 48 automated tests and a 60-case English/Tagalog/Taglish AI evaluation, run in GitHub Actions.

Stack: Laravel 13 · PHP 8.3 · MySQL/SQLite · Tailwind CSS 4 · Gemini API. Built by Lance Ashley Agua.
